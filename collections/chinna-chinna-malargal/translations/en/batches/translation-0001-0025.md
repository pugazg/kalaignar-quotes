## KQ-CCM-0004

- Canonical Tamil: [`0004.md`](../../../quotes/0004.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Speak with measure; but do not take that as a call to measure! Think before you speak; but do not speak everything you think!

**Translation note:** The Tamil deliberately repeats the roots `அளந்து / அளக்க` (“measure”) and `நினைத்து / நினைத்ததை` (“think”). The English keeps the parallel admonition even though the first wordplay is less idiomatic.

## KQ-CCM-0005

- Canonical Tamil: [`0005.md`](../../../quotes/0005.md)

> Virtue, wealth and pleasure are not the only three; some say there is also “veedu”—that is what they call “moksha”!

**Translation note:** The source turns on `வீடு`: first as the term it equates with `மோட்சம்`, then immediately in the ordinary sense of a home.

## KQ-CCM-0011

- Canonical Tamil: [`0011.md`](../../../quotes/0011.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> When justice (`நீதி`) bends to power (`அதிகாரம்`), the first letter of the Tamil word for power joins it and turns it into `அநீதி`—injustice!

**Translation note:** The source wordplay is letter-based: the initial `அ` of `அதிகாரம்` placed before `நீதி` produces `அநீதி`. English cannot reproduce that formation without keeping the Tamil terms visible.

## KQ-CCM-0014

- Canonical Tamil: [`0014.md`](../../../quotes/0014.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> In the “destiny” of this movement envisioned by Anna lies the destiny of each one of us. This movement does not stand with its head raised because of the “destiny” of any one individual among us. If we work with that understanding, those who think of testing themselves against us will tire and sit down.

**Translation note:** The Tamil deliberately plays across the source-visible forms `“தலை எழுத்து”`, `“தலையெழுத்து”` and `தலைதூக்கி`. The first two carry the idiomatic sense of **destiny**, while the final expression literally retains the image of raising the head.
