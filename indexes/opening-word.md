## Index-key method

- The key is derived from the first whitespace-delimited word of the canonical quote transcription.
- Leading/trailing quotation marks or punctuation are omitted from the **index key only**; canonical quote text is never changed.
- No spelling, sandhi, or lexical normalization is performed.
- Entries are grouped by the visible initial Tamil letter.
- Within each initial-letter group, this construction pass orders the literal Unicode opening-word keys for reproducibility. A final Tamil-collation review will be performed after all 497 quotes are covered.
- PDF and printed-page values are copied from the canonical quote files.
