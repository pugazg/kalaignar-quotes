This is the working terminology and style record for the English translation of `chinna-chinna-malargal`.

- Working glossary version: **0.1**

|---|---|---|
| `அறிஞர் அண்ணா` | **Arignar Anna** | Retain the source-facing honorific/name when the honorific is printed; use **Anna** where the source says only `அண்ணா`. |

## Pilot wordplay decisions

### `KQ-CCM-0004`

The source repeats the roots `அளந்து / அளக்க` and `நினைத்து / நினைத்ததை`. English should retain the parallel admonition even though the first wordplay is less natural.

Pilot: **“Speak with measure; but do not take that as a call to measure! Think before you speak; but do not speak everything you think!”**

### `KQ-CCM-0005`

The source turns on `வீடு` as the term it says some call `மோட்சம்`, followed immediately by `வீடு` in the ordinary sense of a home. Keep `veedu` visible in the first clause and explicitly reveal **home** in the second.

### `KQ-CCM-0011`

English cannot reproduce the formation `அ` + `நீதி` → `அநீதி`. Retain the Tamil terms in the English sentence and state the letter operation directly.

### `KQ-CCM-0014`

The source deliberately alternates `தலை எழுத்து`, `தலையெழுத்து` and `தலைதூக்கி`. Translate the governing meaning as **destiny**, preserve **head raised** for `தலைதூக்கி`, and record the wordplay in a translation note.

### `KQ-CCM-0025`

The source juxtaposes `ரசம்` and `விரசம்`. The pilot uses **flavour** and **vulgarity** for readable English and records the Tamil pair in a note rather than pretending the pun survives unchanged.

## General note policy

A translation note should explain only what the English otherwise cannot carry: source wordplay, an intentionally retained source term, or a source uncertainty. It must not become a historical commentary layer.
