## Locked pilot conventions

The following decisions are now the default translation conventions unless later canonical evidence requires an explicit revision:

- `அறிஞர் அண்ணா` → **Arignar Anna** when the honorific is printed; **Anna** when only `அண்ணா` is printed.
- `இலட்சியம்` → **ideal** by default; do not import an unnamed cause.
- `கொள்கை` → **principle**, with **conviction** allowed where the sentence specifically stresses depth or firmness.
- `இயக்கம்` → **movement** unless the canonical text itself names a more specific organization.
- `பொதுவாழ்வு` → **public life**; `பொதுத் தொண்டு / பொதுவேலை` → **public service / public work** according to the printed wording.
- `மோட்சம்` → **moksha**; `அஷ்டமா சித்து` → **ashtama siddhis**; `புனுகு / ஜவ்வாது` → **punugu / javvadu**; `எருக்கம்` → **erukkam** when retained source-facing terminology is necessary.
- Tamil wordplay that cannot survive transparently in English must remain inspectable through retained Tamil terms or a short translation note, not through invented explanatory wording inside the translation.
- `சூடு சொரணை` is handled idiomatically as **sense of shame and self-respect** in the reviewed pilot context; literal heat imagery is not mandatory when the phrase functions idiomatically.
- The quoted `கடிதோச்சி மெல்ல எறிக` is rendered **“Raise swiftly; strike gently”** without supplying an object from outside the canonical quotation.
- `ரசம் / விரசம்` in `KQ-CCM-0025` is rendered **flavour / acrimony**, with the Tamil sound-play documented in a note.

## Style lock

The pilot confirms the following general style:

1. readable English, but source-bound rather than adaptive;
2. no summarising or omission of substantive propositions;
3. preserve rhetorical force, repetition, satire, accusation and metaphor;
4. do not strengthen or soften the Tamil;
5. use short translator notes only for wordplay, retained source terms or source uncertainty;
6. do not introduce historical, doctrinal or political context not present in the canonical quote;
7. preserve traceability to the canonical Tamil file for every quote.
