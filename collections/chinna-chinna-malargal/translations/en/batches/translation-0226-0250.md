## KQ-CCM-0230

- Canonical Tamil: [`0230.md`](../../../quotes/0230.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Those who amass wealth by taking bribes and engaging in smuggling, then take a little of it and perform *thaamam*: if they are caught, they are thieves! If they are not caught, they are great patrons of giving!

**Translation note:** The canonical, scan-verified source prints `தாமம்`. Its intended sense is not independently established by the canonical wording, so the English retains **thaamam** rather than silently normalizing it to another Tamil word. The later `கொடை வள்ளல்கள்` contrast is rendered directly as “great patrons of giving.”
