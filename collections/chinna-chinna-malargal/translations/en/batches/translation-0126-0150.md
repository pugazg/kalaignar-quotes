## KQ-CCM-0126

- Canonical Tamil: [`0126.md`](../../../quotes/0126.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Even if the majority calls a kottan a cuckoo, and a cuckoo a kottan, the kottan will not become a cuckoo, nor will the cuckoo become a kottan!

**Translation note:** The source animal term `கோட்டான்` is retained as **kottan** rather than assigned an external species identity not supplied by the canonical quote. `குயில்` is rendered as **cuckoo**.

## KQ-CCM-0142

- Canonical Tamil: [`0142.md`](../../../quotes/0142.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Does reading palm lines bring any benefit? Certainly—to police investigators!

**Translation note:** The joke turns on `கைரேகை`: in the question it points to palm-line fortune reading, while the answer shifts toward the hand/fingerprint evidence useful to police investigation. English has no single ordinary term that carries both senses, so the lexical pivot is recorded here rather than silently replaced.
