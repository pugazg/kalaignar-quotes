## KQ-CCM-0201

- Canonical Tamil: [`0201.md`](../../../quotes/0201.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Those who possess the capacity for action alone, without eloquence, are like a head without a crown! Those who possess eloquence alone, without the capacity for action, are like a crown without a head! (Here, `mudi` means a crown.)

**Translation note:** The source explicitly clarifies that `முடி` is being used in the sense of **crown**. English therefore preserves the head/crown reversal rather than translating the word as hair.

## KQ-CCM-0202

- Canonical Tamil: [`0202.md`](../../../quotes/0202.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Living by falsehood may seem like unearthing a treasure! As time goes on, that very thing will become the grave-pit of that life!

**Translation note:** The source turns on the related forms `புதையல்` (buried treasure) and `புதைகுழி` (burial pit/grave). English preserves the treasure/grave contrast but cannot reproduce the Tamil root relation exactly.

## KQ-CCM-0213

> Attachment means having deep love for something, even making sacrifices to protect it. ## KQ-CCM-0223

- Canonical Tamil: [`0223.md`](../../../quotes/0223.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Thanthai Periyar, who spoke in support of the DMK government and expressed his wish that it remain and endure, said that among “duty, dignity and discipline” in the DMK, even the first two should be pushed behind, and that the third—“discipline”—should be the first thing that the Kazhagam observes.
