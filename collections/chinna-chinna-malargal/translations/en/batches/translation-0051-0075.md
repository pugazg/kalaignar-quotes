## KQ-CCM-0051

- Canonical Tamil: [`0051.md`](../../../quotes/0051.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Even among flowers there is one called “saathi malligai.” It seems that, by looking at human beings, even the spotless community of flowers has been corrupted!

**Translation note:** The satire turns on the printed flower name `சாதி மல்லிகை`: `சாதி` is also the Tamil word for **caste**. The source-facing flower name is retained rather than replaced by an externally supplied botanical label.
