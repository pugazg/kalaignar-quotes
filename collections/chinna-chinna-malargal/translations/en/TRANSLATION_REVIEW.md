## Reviewed correction ledger

### `KQ-CCM-0021`

**Reviewed English**

> Within a movement, garlands of praise themselves come running in search of the service-minded person who runs in search of public work!

**Reason:** Retains the source's repeated `தேடி ஓட` / running-in-search image while removing the first-pass English relative-clause tangle.

### `KQ-CCM-0041`

**Reviewed English**

> Our heart finds greater comfort in feeling twice the joy at seeing the good that comes to someone through being at enmity with us than in the joy we feel at the good that comes to someone who is friendly with us.

**Reason:** English syntax clarified; the unusual source contrast between benefit through friendship and benefit through enmity is not normalized away.

### `KQ-CCM-0306`

**Reviewed English**

> Anna is a Mamallapuram sculpture carved into the mountain-rock called Periyar! All of us are replicas of that sculpture!

**Reason:** `மாதிரி வடிவங்கள்` is more naturally carried by **replicas** than the first-pass phrase “model forms,” with no change to the metaphor.

### `KQ-CCM-0310`

**Reviewed English**

> Even rocks melted! Even deserts poured forth tears! Yet this wretched world alone would not turn its face toward ending women's grief of widowhood!

**Reason:** Recasts the first-pass “widow's grief of women” into natural English while preserving `பாவையரின் விதவைத் துயர்` and the source's rhetorical force.

### `KQ-CCM-0364`

**Reviewed English**

> If the name “national mainstream” is given to the drama being staged — with syrup-sweet words such as “As long as the non-Hindi-speaking states wish, there will be no imposition of Hindi,” while carefully, gradually, artfully and gracefully pursuing the goal that “Hindi alone will one day be India’s single language” — is it not wrong even to show one’s face near that theatre?

**Reason:** The canonical scan reads `விரும்பும் வரையில்`. The first pass supplied “do not want it” to make the quotation idiomatic; the review removes that inserted negation and keeps the printed formulation source-bound.

### `KQ-CCM-0424`

**Reviewed English**

> Tamils by language — Dravidians as a people — Indians by country — human beings in the world: that is our movement’s idea! It is worth thinking that Kaniyan, the Sangam-age poet who sang “Yadhum oore, yaavarum kelir” (“Every place is our town; everyone our kin”), joined the name of his own village Poongundram to his name and was called “Kaniyan Poongundranar.”

**Reason:** Replaces the unidiomatic first-pass “Dravidians by people” with **Dravidians as a people**, preserving `இனத்தால் திராவிடர்` without introducing a biological-race formulation.

### `KQ-CCM-0437`

**Reviewed English**

> It is the DMK that raised its voice to protect democracy, for the rights needed for the welfare of the Tamil people, and for social justice; that stood and still stands on the path of principle even when its government was dismissed twice because of this, and when ten legislators were stripped of their posts in the struggle against Hindi domination! When I think that I am a part of this, there is no equal to the joy I feel!

**Reason:** Corrects the first-pass English syntax “the posts of ten legislators were stripped” without changing the source claim.

### `KQ-CCM-0441`

**Reviewed English**

> Are all those who scatter groundnuts by the handful, while secretly drinking up the whole sea and belching without those who pick up and eat the groundnuts knowing it, great benefactors?

**Translation note:** Tamil plays on `கடலை` (groundnut) and `கடலையே` (the sea).

**Reason:** Preserves the source's groundnut/sea wordplay while removing an awkward nested first-pass clause.

### `KQ-CCM-0461`

**Reviewed English**

> The gold-mine worker and the pearl diver make no ornament for themselves from gold or pearls; only the weaver makes a garment for himself — because he places such great value on honour!

**Reason:** Restores the weaver as the explicit subject of the closing clause (`மானத்துக்கு அவ்வளவு மதிப்பு அளிப்பதால்`) rather than using the impersonal first-pass “such value is placed.”

### `KQ-CCM-0484`

**Reviewed English**

> We split a mountain into stones. From one stone we make an idol. The other stones become halls. They become towers. They become enclosing walls. Not every stone can become an idol. If they all did, there could be no temple. The stone that is the idol must not forget that it too came from the mountain just like the other stones. Only because the temple exists does the idol receive *abhishekam* and worship! Otherwise, under which tree or at which bathing steps would it lie? This is a truth that those in every position of responsibility in an organization must understand!

**Reason:** The final sentence is recast from the awkward first-pass “everyone holding every responsibility” to the source-faithful **those in every position of responsibility**. The rest of the first-pass translation is retained.

### `KQ-CCM-0495`

**Reviewed English**

> If a wall infested by termites is left unattended, it will collapse without even needing a crowbar to demolish it! The fate of a tainted government is the same.

**Reason:** `கரைபடிந்த ஓர் ஆட்சி` supports a **tainted / stained government**. The first pass added “by corruption,” which the canonical wording does not explicitly state. That unsupported specification is removed.

## Consistency decisions confirmed

The full pass retains the locked glossary/style system. In particular:

- `இலட்சியம்` remains **ideal** by default; no later batch requires a change to the pilot convention.
- `கொள்கை` remains **principle / conviction** according to sentence force.
- `பொதுவாழ்வு` remains **public life** and generic `இயக்கம்` remains **movement** unless the source names a movement.
- `திராவிட இயக்கம்` remains **Dravidian Movement**; `தி.மு.க.` remains **DMK**; generic `கழகம்` / `கழக ஆட்சி` remains source-facing **Kazhagam / Kazhagam government**.
- Source honorific distinctions remain visible: **Anna**, **Arignar Anna**, **Perarignar Anna**, **Periyar**, and **Thanthai Periyar** follow the printed source form.
- `வகுப்புவாதம்` remains **vaguppuvaadam** rather than being silently mapped to a later English political category.
- Ordinary `பற்று` remains distinct from intensified `வெறி`; the latter is rendered as **fanaticism / frenzy** where the source itself intensifies the term.
- Culturally or lexically source-bound forms such as **kottan**, **poikkaal kuthirai**, **pirmahathi**, **thazhampoo**, **vilangu fish**, **kudukuduppai**, **thaamam**, **kavadi**, **hundial**, **avai arithal**, **oodhukaamalai**, **kol**, **onaan**, **vaananaigal**, **punithamodathai**, **mukkudaipattaalum** and **amirtham** remain source-facing rather than being reconstructed from outside material.
- Tamil wordplay remains visible through retained source terms or short notes where English cannot reproduce it directly.
