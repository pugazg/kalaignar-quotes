## Pilot review result

All 25 pilot translations were compared against their canonical audited Tamil files. Four English renderings were refined for fidelity/readability: `KQ-CCM-0004`, `KQ-CCM-0016`, `KQ-CCM-0020` and `KQ-CCM-0025`. The remaining 21 were retained. Details and rationale are recorded in [`PILOT_REVIEW.md`](PILOT_REVIEW.md).

The pilot convention lock is now version **1.0**. In particular:

- `சூடு சொரணை` is handled idiomatically as **sense of shame and self-respect** in the reviewed pilot context;
- `கடிதோச்சி மெல்ல எறிக` is **“Raise swiftly; strike gently”** without importing an unstated object;
- `ரசம் / விரசம்` in `KQ-CCM-0025` is **flavour / acrimony** with the Tamil wordplay documented;
- source-facing terms such as **moksha**, **ashtama siddhis**, **punugu**, **javvadu** and **erukkam** remain governed by the locked glossary.
