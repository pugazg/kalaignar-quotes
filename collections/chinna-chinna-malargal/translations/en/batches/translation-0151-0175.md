## KQ-CCM-0151

- Canonical Tamil: [`0151.md`](../../../quotes/0151.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Even in the homes of those who proclaim, “At my mother’s lap I drank Tamil milk, spoke my infant speech in Tamil; this body is for the soil—this life for Tamil alone,” if, when it comes to a wedding, bending and bowing before *vadamozhi* is considered respect, when will this Tamil people hold its head high?

**Translation note:** The source term `வடமொழி` is retained as **vadamozhi** (“northern language”) rather than silently expanded to a more specific language identity not printed in the canonical quote. `தமிழ்ச்சாதி` is rendered contextually as **Tamil people** without importing a biological race sense.

## KQ-CCM-0162

- Canonical Tamil: [`0162.md`](../../../quotes/0162.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Only when the Venus called discernment rises and the Saturn called caste-madness recedes will the Sun called equality appear!

**Translation note:** The Tamil sequence `வெள்ளி / சனி / ஞாயிறு` carries a wordplay that English cannot reproduce directly: the terms denote Venus, Saturn and the Sun while also naming Friday, Saturday and Sunday. The translation keeps the celestial imagery and records the lost weekday sequence here.
