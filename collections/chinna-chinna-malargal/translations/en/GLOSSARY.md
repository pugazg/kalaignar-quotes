## Locked pilot wordplay decisions

### `KQ-CCM-0004`

The source repeats the roots `அளந்து / அளக்க` and `நினைத்து / நினைத்ததை`. English should retain the parallel admonition while acknowledging that the first root-play cannot be reproduced exactly.

Locked rendering: **“Speak with measure; but don't be calculating about it! Think before you speak; but do not speak everything you think!”**

### `KQ-CCM-0005`

The source turns on `வீடு` as the term it says some call `மோட்சம்`, followed immediately by `வீடு` in the ordinary sense of a home. Keep `veedu` visible in the first clause and explicitly reveal **home** in the second.

### `KQ-CCM-0011`

English cannot reproduce the formation `அ` + `நீதி` → `அநீதி`. Retain the Tamil terms in the English sentence and state the letter operation directly.

### `KQ-CCM-0014`

The source deliberately alternates `தலை எழுத்து`, `தலையெழுத்து` and `தலைதூக்கி`. Translate the governing meaning as **destiny**, preserve **head raised** for `தலைதூக்கி`, and record the wordplay in a translation note.

### `KQ-CCM-0016`

The reviewed pilot treats `சூடு சொரணை` idiomatically as **sense of shame and self-respect**. The preceding `இரத்தம் கொதிப்பதை` already carries the source's explicit heat image; English should not create a second literal-fire image from the idiom.

### `KQ-CCM-0020`

The quoted words `கடிதோச்சி மெல்ல எறிக` are rendered **“Raise swiftly; strike gently”**. Do not add an object from external Kural context unless the canonical quotation itself supplies one.

### `KQ-CCM-0025`

The source juxtaposes `ரசம்` and `விரசம்`. The reviewed rendering uses **flavour** and **acrimony** for readable political-discourse English and records the Tamil pair in a note rather than pretending the pun survives unchanged.

## Batch-2 wordplay / source-term decisions

### `KQ-CCM-0037`

The source places `மேய்ப்போன்` and `எய்ப்போன்` in a close sound relationship. Use **shepherd / deceiver** for the semantic contrast and record that the Tamil sound-play is not reproducible rather than manufacturing an English pun.

### `KQ-CCM-0042`

Translate `திராவிட இயக்கம்` and `சுயமரியாதை மாநாடு` directly, but retain `கழக` as **Kazhagam**. The canonical quote provides its own dates and legislative claim; translation must not add party identification or external history.

### `KQ-CCM-0045`

The Tamil sequence uses dense sound-patterning across `தேன் / தேனிசை`, `மான் / மீன்`, `நாணம் / மானம் / மாண்பு`. Preserve the ordered images and semantic contrasts; use a short note to acknowledge the sound pattern rather than distorting the English to chase rhyme.

### `KQ-CCM-0050`

Retain `அறிவியக்கம்` as **Ariviyakkam**. Do not silently identify it with an external institution, movement name or doctrinal label beyond what the canonical quote itself says.

## General translation style lock

1. Translate every substantive proposition; do not summarise.
2. Prefer natural English syntax where needed for intelligibility, but do not add interpretation absent from the canonical Tamil.
3. Preserve accusation, satire, ridicule, rhetorical questions, exclamation, repetition and metaphor without softening or intensifying them.
4. Do not import historical, doctrinal, party or ideological identification unless the canonical quote states it.
5. Culturally specific terms may remain transliterated when a supposedly smoother English replacement would add unsupported meaning.
6. Use a translation note only for source wordplay, a deliberately retained source term or source uncertainty that English cannot otherwise carry.
7. Every translation must remain traceable to the canonical Tamil quote file.
8. Canonical Tamil wording, punctuation, provenance and verification status are never changed as part of translation work.
