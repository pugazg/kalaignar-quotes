## Batch 2 notes

`KQ-CCM-0026`–`KQ-CCM-0050` were translated directly from the 25 canonical audited Tamil files. All 25 source states are `verified_from_scan`.

Source-facing additions recorded in glossary version **1.1** include:

- `தமிழ் உணர்வு` → **Tamil consciousness**;
- `திராவிட இயக்கம்` → **Dravidian Movement**;
- `சுயமரியாதை மாநாடு` → **Self-Respect Conference**;
- `கழக ஆட்சி` → **Kazhagam government**, without silently expanding the source into an unstated party name;
- `பெரியார்` → **Periyar** and `பேரறிஞர் அண்ணா` → **Perarignar Anna**;
- `அறிவியக்கம்` → **Ariviyakkam**, retained as a source term rather than assigned an external organizational or doctrinal identity.

The batch also records translation notes where Tamil sound-play materially cannot be reproduced in English, especially `KQ-CCM-0037` (`மேய்ப்போன் / எய்ப்போன்`) and `KQ-CCM-0045` (`தேன் / தேனிசை`, `மான் / மீன்`, `நாணம் / மானம் / மாண்பு`).

No OCR text, online quotation or outside historical reconstruction was used as translation authority. Canonical Tamil text, punctuation, page provenance and verification states remain untouched.
