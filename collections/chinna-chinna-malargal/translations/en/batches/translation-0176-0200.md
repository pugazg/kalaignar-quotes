## KQ-CCM-0182

- Canonical Tamil: [`0182.md`](../../../quotes/0182.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> Those in public life should regard newspapers as full-length mirrors. They too should be mirrors with a reflective coating. They must not be mirrors coated with *virasam*—acrimony!

**Translation note:** The source deliberately plays across two earlier source-facing senses: mirror-context `ரசம் பூசுதல்` is the **reflective coating** convention already established in the glossary, while `விரசம்` echoes `ரசம்` and carries an acrimonious/distasteful sense. English cannot reproduce the Tamil sound relation, so **virasam** is kept visible.
