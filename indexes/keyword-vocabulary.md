## Controlled keywords

|---|---|---|
| `abuse-of-power` | அதிகார முறைகேடு | Misuse, distortion or improper exercise of authority or official power. Use when power itself is materially abused, not merely because government or office is mentioned. |
| `anarchy` | அராஜகம் | Explicit disorder or lawless public condition described as `அராஜகம்` or its direct equivalent. |

| `authoritarianism` | சர்வாதிகாரம் | Autocratic or dictatorial conduct in which one actor seeks exclusive superiority or control. |
| `basic-needs` | அடிப்படைத் தேவைகள் | Minimum material necessities explicitly treated together, such as food, clothing and housing. Do not use for a single isolated need when the quote does not frame it as part of a minimum-needs set. |
| `character` | உள்ளப் பண்பு | Inner personal quality or moral character contrasted with outward appearance or surface impression. |
| `civility` | உரையாடல் நாகரிகம் | Restraint from degrading, vulgar or needlessly hostile expression in public or political exchange. |
| `class-dominance` | வர்க்க ஆதிக்கம் | Dominance or protected power of a social class or group explicitly described as a `வர்க்கம்`. |
| `collective-identity` | கூட்டு அடையாளம் | The individual's identity, fate or standing understood as inseparable from a larger collective or movement. |
| `corruption` | ஊழல் | Explicit corruption, corrupt practice or corrupt public condition. |
| `deception` | ஏமாற்றுதல் | Deliberate misleading or trickery where deception itself is material to the proposition. |
| `discipline` | கட்டுப்பாடு | Explicit organizational or personal discipline that regulates collective functioning. |
| `empathy` | பிறருணர்வு | Sharing or identifying with another person's joy, suffering or emotional condition. |
| `exclusion` | ஒதுக்குமுறை | A claim that others should be denied equal standing, recognition or place while one actor alone is elevated. |
| `experience` | அனுபவம் | Experience, especially past or lived experience, used as a source of learning, judgment or orientation. |
| `freedom` | சுதந்திரம் | Explicit freedom or independence as a political, civic or social condition. Distinguish from `rights`, which indexes explicit claims to entitlements or rights. |

| `government` | அரசு | Government as an acting institution whose conduct, restraint or responsibility is materially discussed. |
| `hierarchy` | படிநிலை ஆதிக்கம் | A superior–subordinate social ordering, especially when one subordinated person seeks another person below them. |
| `housing` | வீடு / குடியிருப்பு | Housing, shelter or the lack of a place to live when materially discussed. |
| `humaneness` | மனிதாபிமானம் | Humaneness, compassion or humane regard explicitly presented as a moral quality. |
| `jealousy` | பொறாமை | Jealousy or envy explicitly identified as an attitude to be avoided or overcome. |
| `justice` | நீதி | Justice as a principle or institutional condition. Use even when the quote describes justice bending or being compromised; use `injustice` only when that negative condition itself is a distinct indexed concept in a later batch. |
| `love` | அன்பு | Love or affectionate concern when materially presented as a moral or relational value. |
| `misuse-of-law` | சட்டத் தவறான பயன்பாடு | Law being used wrongly, improperly or as an instrument of abuse. More specific than the broad `justice` key. |
| `moksha` | மோட்சம் | `மோட்சம்` / liberation as an explicitly named religious concept. |

| `oppression` | ஒடுக்குமுறை | A group or community being kept down, suppressed or subordinated. |
| `organization` | அமைப்பு முறை | Organizational structure, arrangement or method that channels collective activity. Distinguish from `movement`, which indexes the collective entity itself. |
| `performance` | அரங்கேற்றம் | Stage debut, performance or public presentation when it is material to evaluating ability. |
| `perseverance` | விடாமுயற்சி | Continuing through obstacles, adverse conditions or difficulty rather than waiting for ideal conditions. |
| `political-discourse` | அரசியல் உரையாடல் | Writing, speech, response and counter-response explicitly situated within political exchange. |
| `press` | ஏடுகள் / பத்திரிகை | Newspapers, journals or the press as actors whose stance or reaction is materially discussed. |

| `public-life` | பொதுவாழ்வு | Participation in public life as a field of civic or political engagement. Distinguish from `public-service`, which requires explicit service/work for the public or collective. |

| `speech` | பேச்சு | Speech or spoken expression when materially discussed as an act, practice or mode of public exchange. |
| `talent` | திறமை / மேதைமை | Ability, talent or potential for excellence when the quote materially evaluates whether it is present. |

## Vocabulary review notes

- The initial vocabulary deliberately favors reusable concepts over indexing every noun or metaphor in the first 25 quotes.
- Broad theme labels such as `human-values-dignity-character` or `political-ethics-power-public-life` are not duplicated here as keywords.
- Named-person indexing was not initialized from `KQ-CCM-0001` or `KQ-CCM-0014` because the named person is not itself the proposition being indexed.
- Nature imagery in the first batch was not automatically turned into one-off keywords where it primarily functions as illustration rather than a reusable subject.

No vocabulary migrations have occurred yet.
