## KQ-CCM-0293

- Canonical Tamil: [`0293.md`](../../../quotes/0293.md)
- Source status: `verified_from_scan`

> When does violence raise its head? When the *mei ezhuthu* is struck and the *uyir ezhuthu* is removed, what remains is the *aytha ezhuthu*, is it not!

**Translation note:** The source builds its sentence from Tamil letter-category names: `மெய் எழுத்து`, `உயிர் எழுத்து` and `ஆய்த எழுத்து`. `மெய்` also carries the sense of body and `உயிர்` the sense of life, so the violence image depends on wordplay that cannot be reproduced transparently in English. The three source terms are therefore kept visible rather than replaced with a smoother reconstruction.
