## Batch 4 notes

`KQ-CCM-0076`–`KQ-CCM-0100` were translated directly from the 25 canonical audited Tamil files. All 25 source states are `verified_from_scan`.

Glossary version **1.3** adds source-bound handling for:

- `வாமனாவதாரம்` → **Vamana avatar** and `மாவலி மன்னன்` → **King Mahabali**, without expanding the named story beyond the quote;
- mirror-context `ரசம் பூசுதல்` → **reflective coating**, kept distinct from the earlier `ரசம் / விரசம்` wordplay;
- `கடையேழு வள்ளல்கள்` → **the Last Seven Great Patrons**, `முல்லைக் கொடி` → **mullai creeper**;
- `பத்தாம் பசலி` → **outmoded** and `பன்னீர்ப்பூ` → **panneer flower** in the source comparison;
- `வெறி` → **fanaticism**, `மொழி ஆதிக்கம்` → **language domination**, and `மத ஆதிக்கம்` → **religious domination** where explicitly stated;
- `வகுப்புவாதம்` → **vaguppuvaadam**, retained rather than silently forced into a later English political category;
- `கள்ளிச் செடி` → **kalli plant** without outside botanical identification;
- `பாதரசம்` → **mercury**, translating the source comparison without treating translation as scientific verification.

Translation notes record the lost `பற்று / பற்றி எரியும்` wordplay in `KQ-CCM-0090`, the source-specific `vaguppuvaadam` handling in `KQ-CCM-0094`, and other terms only where English cannot transparently carry the Tamil.

No OCR text, online quotation or outside historical reconstruction was used as translation authority. Canonical Tamil text, punctuation, page provenance and verification states remain untouched.
